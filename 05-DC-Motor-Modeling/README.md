
# DC Motor Modeling

## Overview
This section presents the mathematical modeling and dynamic analysis of DC motors as fundamental electromechanical systems.

The electrical and mechanical subsystems are combined to develop a complete dynamic model of a DC motor.

## Topics
- Introduction to DC Motors
- Basic Operating Principles
- Electrical Equivalent Circuit
- Armature Voltage and Current
- Back Electromotive Force (Back EMF)
- Electromagnetic Torque
- Rotor Inertia and Viscous Friction
- Electrical and Mechanical Equations
- Transfer Function Modeling
- Open-Loop Dynamic Response
- MATLAB/Simulink Simulation

## Learning Outcomes
Students will be able to:
- Explain the operation of DC motors.
- Derive the electrical and mechanical equations.
- Develop a coupled electromechanical model.
- Obtain transfer functions.
- Analyze motor speed and position responses.
- Simulate open-loop motor dynamics.

DC motor models and open-loop simulations will be added throughout the course.
