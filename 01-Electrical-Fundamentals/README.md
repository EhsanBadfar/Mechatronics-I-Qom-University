
# Electrical Fundamentals

## Overview
This section introduces the fundamental concepts of electrical engineering required for the analysis and modeling of mechatronic systems.

The main objective is to understand the behavior of basic electrical components and develop mathematical models of electrical circuits.

## Topics
- Introduction to Electrical Engineering
- Voltage, Current, and Electrical Power
- Resistors, Inductors, and Capacitors (R, L, C)
- Ohm's Law
- Kirchhoff's Voltage and Current Laws
- Series and Parallel Circuits
- RC, RL, and RLC Circuits
- Mathematical Modeling of Electrical Circuits
- Transient Response of Electrical Systems

## Learning Outcomes
Students will be able to:
- Identify basic electrical components.
- Analyze series and parallel circuits.
- Apply Kirchhoff's laws.
- Derive differential equations for RC, RL, and RLC circuits.
- Simulate the dynamic response of electrical systems.
