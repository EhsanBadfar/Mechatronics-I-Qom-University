
# Mechatronics I

### University of Qom
**Course Materials, MATLAB Codes, and Simulation Examples**

---

## Course Overview

Mechatronics I introduces the fundamental principles of mechatronic systems, with an emphasis on the integration of electrical engineering, mechanical dynamics, sensors, actuators, and control systems.

The course begins with the analysis of basic electrical and mechanical components, followed by mathematical modeling and simulation of practical engineering systems, including vehicle suspension systems, automotive airbag systems, and DC motors.

The final part of the course introduces sensors, feedback control, and microcontrollers, culminating in the modeling, simulation, and control of DC motor position and speed.

This repository provides lecture materials, MATLAB codes, simulation models, and practical examples developed throughout the course.

## Course Objectives

By the end of this course, students are expected to be able to:

- Understand the fundamental principles of electrical and mechanical systems.
- Analyze basic electrical circuits containing resistors, inductors, and capacitors.
- Develop mathematical models of mechanical and electromechanical systems.
- Model and simulate practical mechatronic applications.
- Understand the operating principles of common sensors and actuators.
- Explain the fundamentals of open-loop and closed-loop control systems.
- Understand the basic architecture and applications of microcontrollers.
- Design and simulate basic position and speed controllers for DC motors.

## Course Syllabus

### Part I – Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering

- Introduction to Electrical Engineering
- Basic Electrical Quantities: Voltage, Current, and Power
- Resistors, Inductors, and Capacitors (R, L, C)
- Series and Parallel Circuit Analysis
- Kirchhoff's Voltage and Current Laws
- Introduction to Electrical System Modeling

### Part II – Fundamentals of Mechanical Systems

- Introduction to Mechanical Systems
- Mass, Spring, and Damper Elements
- Newton's Laws of Motion
- Mathematical Modeling of Mechanical Systems
- Mass–Spring–Damper Systems
- Analogies Between Electrical and Mechanical Systems

### Part III – Modeling of Mechatronic Systems

**1. Vehicle Suspension System**
- Mathematical Modeling
- Dynamic Analysis
- MATLAB/Simulink Simulation

**2. Automotive Airbag System**
- Introduction to Airbag Systems
- Simplified Impact Dynamics
- Acceleration Sensing and Deployment Logic
- Mathematical Modeling and Simulation

**3. DC Motor Modeling**
- Electrical Dynamics of DC Motors
- Mechanical Dynamics of DC Motors
- Coupled Electromechanical Modeling
- Open-Loop Response Analysis
- MATLAB/Simulink Simulation

### Part IV – Introduction to Sensors

- Fundamentals of Sensors and Measurement Systems
- Position Sensors
- Speed Sensors
- Acceleration Sensors
- Sensor Applications in Mechatronic Systems

### Part V – Fundamentals of Control Systems

- Introduction to Control Systems
- Open-Loop and Closed-Loop Control
- Feedback Control Principles
- Introduction to PID Controllers
- Basic Control System Simulation

### Part VI – Introduction to Microcontrollers

- Fundamentals of Microcontrollers
- Microcontroller Architecture
- Digital and Analog Inputs and Outputs
- Pulse Width Modulation (PWM)
- Interfacing Sensors and Actuators

### Part VII – DC Motor Position and Speed Control

- DC Motor Speed Control
- DC Motor Position Control
- Feedback Control Implementation
- PID Controller Design
- MATLAB/Simulink Simulation
- Introduction to Microcontroller-Based Motor Control

## How to Use This Repository

1. Select the topic from the repository structure.
2. Open the corresponding folder.
3. Read the instructions provided for each example.
4. Download the MATLAB scripts or Simulink models.
5. Run the examples and analyze the simulation results.

To download the entire repository, select **Code → Download ZIP**.
