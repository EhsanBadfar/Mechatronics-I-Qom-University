
# Automotive Airbag System

## Overview
This section introduces the basic operating principles and simplified mathematical modeling of automotive airbag systems.

The emphasis is on vehicle impact dynamics, acceleration sensing, and the conceptual role of airbags in occupant protection.

## Topics
- Introduction to Automotive Safety Systems
- Basic Principles of Airbag Operation
- Vehicle Impact Dynamics
- Acceleration and Deceleration Analysis
- Simplified Mass-Spring-Damper Modeling
- Impact Force and Energy Absorption
- Acceleration Sensors
- Conceptual Airbag Deployment Logic
- Mathematical Modeling and Simulation

## Learning Outcomes
Students will be able to:
- Explain the basic operation of airbag systems.
- Analyze simplified impact dynamics.
- Develop elementary mechanical models.
- Investigate acceleration and impact forces.
- Understand the role of sensors in safety systems.

## MATLAB/Simulink Examples
Educational simulation models of simplified impact dynamics and acceleration responses will be provided.

## Safety Note
All models are intended for educational purposes only and must not be used for real-world automotive safety system design or deployment.
