
# Vehicle Suspension System

## Overview
This section focuses on the mathematical modeling and dynamic analysis of automotive suspension systems.

A simplified quarter-car suspension model is introduced to investigate vehicle vibration, ride comfort, and suspension performance.

## Topics
- Introduction to Vehicle Suspension Systems
- Suspension Components
- Springs and Shock Absorbers
- Quarter-Car Suspension Models
- Free-Body Diagrams
- Equations of Motion
- Road Disturbance Modeling
- Dynamic Response Analysis
- MATLAB/Simulink Modeling

## Learning Outcomes
Students will be able to:
- Explain the operation of suspension systems.
- Develop simplified suspension models.
- Derive the governing differential equations.
- Simulate vehicle responses to road disturbances.
- Investigate the effects of stiffness and damping.

## MATLAB/Simulink Examples
Examples of quarter-car suspension modeling, road disturbances, and vibration analysis will be added.
