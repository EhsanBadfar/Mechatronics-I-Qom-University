
# Mechanical Fundamentals

## Overview
This section introduces the fundamental principles of mechanical dynamics and mathematical modeling of mechanical systems.

Special emphasis is placed on mass, spring, and damper elements and their similarities to electrical components.

## Topics
- Introduction to Mechanical Systems
- Newton's Laws of Motion
- Mass, Spring, and Damper Elements
- Free-Body Diagrams
- Equation of Motion
- Mass-Spring-Damper Systems
- Free and Forced Vibrations
- Damping and Dynamic Response
- Electrical-Mechanical Analogies

## Learning Outcomes
Students will be able to:
- Identify basic mechanical elements.
- Construct free-body diagrams.
- Derive equations of motion.
- Model mass-spring-damper systems.
- Analyze mechanical system responses.
- Understand electrical-mechanical analogies.

## MATLAB/Simulink Examples
Simulation examples of mechanical systems, including mass-spring-damper models, will be provided.
